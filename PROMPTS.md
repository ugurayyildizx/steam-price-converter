# Steam Price Converter Logo & Promo Prompts

## Extension Logo (App Icon)
**Prompt:**
> A modern and sleek circular icon for a browser extension named "Steam Price Converter". The design should feature a stylized currency exchange symbol (like two arrows forming a circle) with a Steam logo (the crank arm) integrated into the center. Use a color palette of Steam Navy (#1b2838), Cyber Blue (#66c0f4), and a hint of Gold (#fdf45f) for the currency element. The style should be flat, minimalist, and look professional at small sizes (128x128). No text.
